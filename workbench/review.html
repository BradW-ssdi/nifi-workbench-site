BA review

Each template with its requirements as numbered notes. Open a template, read its notes beside it, and use Show on template to find each one on the screen. The full process, from Ready for BA review to Approved, is in docs/ba-review.md.

  1. Pick a template from the table. Templates for November 1 come first, in roadmap order.
  2. Check each note against the business rules. A note marked Question for BA needs an answer.
  3. For a change, an answer, or an action, use Add feedback on the note (or Add feedback on the whole template). It is saved as a comment on the template's Jira story and shows here under the note. An action has an owner and a due date and can be marked done. If feedback here is not connected yet, use Copy reference on the note and paste it into your comment on the story (for example "Merge Patients: Manual Search, note MP-5").
  4. When the notes are right, add feedback on the whole template with the type Approval (or comment Approved on the story). Design then marks the template Approved here, with the date, and it goes to development.
