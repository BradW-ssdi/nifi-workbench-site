App Frame

The shared frame every screen sits in: skip link, main navigation rail, header, and footer. Nothing on this page is screen-specific.

What to check against this page

  1. Press Tab once from the address bar: "Skip to main content" appears; Enter moves focus to the page content.
  2. The rail starts collapsed (icons only). Hover or Tab to an icon: its label appears beside it. Escape hides the label.
  3. The menu button expands and collapses the rail. The choice persists while you move between screens in the same tab.
  4. Data Entry and Merge Patients open and close their lists. Clicking either one while the rail is collapsed expands the rail and opens that list.
  5. The logo returns to Home. Help and the account menu work from the keyboard; Escape closes the account menu and returns focus to its button.
  6. The header stays at the top while the page scrolls; the footer sits at the bottom of short pages.
  7. At 200% and 400% zoom the rail becomes a bar with the menu button, and nothing scrolls sideways.
